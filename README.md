# Nota Ryan Mobile Notary

Build a professional, classic-style business website for NotaRy Mobile Notary, owned by Ryan Mancini, located in Braddock, Pennsylvania.



Business Details



- Business Name: NotaRy Mobile Notary

- Owner: Ryan Mancini

- Phone: (412) 607-5670

- Location: Braddock, Pennsylvania, USA



Design Requirements



- Classic American professional design.

- Navy blue, ivory white, and subtle gold color palette.

- Elegant typography, clean layouts, professional notary-themed visuals, and premium appearance.

- Fully responsive for mobile, tablet, and desktop.

- Smooth scrolling, subtle animations, and professional hover effects.



Website Sections



1. Header with logo, navigation, and Call Now button.

2. Hero section with headline: “Professional Notary Services That Come to You.”

3. About Ryan Mancini section.

4. Services section with editable service cards.

5. How It Works section.

6. Frequently Asked Questions.

7. Contact section with phone number and location.

8. Appointment request form with name, phone, email, requested service, preferred date, preferred time, and message.

9. Footer with contact information, navigation, and privacy policy link.



Functionality



- Call Now button must call (412) 607-5670 using a clickable phone link.

- Appointment form should validate required fields and display a clear success message after submission.

- For now, the form can use frontend-only validation and a demo success message. Do not connect a database.

- Do not build an admin panel, login page, authentication system, CRM, or backend.

- Do not invent business hours, prices, email addresses, street addresses, testimonials, certifications, or unconfirmed services.

- Keep the design and code organized so a database and admin panel can be added later without rebuilding the entire website.



Include the required Pennsylvania notary advertising disclaimer:



“I am not an attorney licensed to practice law in this Commonwealth. I am not allowed to draft legal records, give advice on legal matters, including immigration, or charge a fee for those activities.”



Build the actual website, not just a mockup. Focus on professional design, easy navigation, mobile responsiveness, and encouraging visitors to call or request an appointment.
